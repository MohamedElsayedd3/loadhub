Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. All downloads, browsing history and bookmarks are stored locally on your device only and are never sent to any server. The app collects no personal data and requires no account or login.

The app shows ads through Google AdMob; AdMob may collect an advertising identifier from your device according to its own privacy policy. You can delete all your data at any time from inside the app (clear history / bookmarks / downloads).

By using the app you agree to the disclaimer below and to this policy. For support or inquiries, please contact us through the app's page on Google Play.
